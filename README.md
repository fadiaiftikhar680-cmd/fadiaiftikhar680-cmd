### 🚀 Featured Projects

#### 1. 📧 [Email Intelligence Platform — Find. Verify. Enrich.](https://github.com/fadiaiftikhar680-cmd/Email-Intelligence)
> *An enterprise-grade, evidence-based email discovery, verification, and company intelligence platform built with strict security and zero-dummy-data architecture.*

- **Core Architecture:** Engineered a **13-rule deterministic verification engine** (DNS, multi-MX failover, 600+ disposable domain filters, role-based detection, and safe SMTP probing) alongside a bounded-depth company & people discovery pipeline.
- **Scale & Security:** Supports asynchronous bulk processing (up to 10,000 CSV/XLSX rows via Celery & Redis), spreadsheet formula-injection neutralization, SSRF outbound guards, Argon2 hashing, and scoped SHA-256 REST APIs with OpenAPI/Swagger UI.
- **Tech Stack:** `Python` · `Django` · `PostgreSQL` · `Redis` · `Celery` · `Docker` · `REST API (OpenAPI 3.0)`

#### 2. 🇵🇰 [E-Commerce Intelligence Dashboard Tool (Pakistan Market)](https://github.com/fadiaiftikhar680-cmd/Ecommerce_Intelligence_Tool)
> *A smart event, unit-economics, and COD return-risk intelligence SaaS platform tailored for the Pakistani e-commerce ecosystem.*

- **Core Architecture:** Built on a decoupled 3-layer architecture synchronizing lunar **Hijri & seasonal sales spikes** (Ramadan, Eid, 11.11, Wedding Season) with **30–45 day seller demand alerts**, winning product discovery, and unit-economics waterfall analytics.
- **Risk & AI Engine:** Features a parcel-level **COD Return (RTO) Risk Predictor** across Pakistani cities and couriers (Trax, PostEx, Leopards, TCS), automated WhatsApp confirmation SOPs, inventory runway tracking, and **Gemini AI** merchant insights.
- **Tech Stack:** `Python` · `Streamlit` · `FastAPI` · `Plotly` · `Pandas` · `Scikit-learn` · `Gemini AI`
